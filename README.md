# 🏨 Luxury Stay Hospitality -MERN Stack Project 

> **A full-stack Hotel Management System (HMS) built with the MERN stack Cotaining both User and Admin Panel**

**"LuxuryStay Hospitality"** is a high-end hotel chain known for its exceptional service and luxurious accommodations. With a network of hotels in various locations, LuxuryStay aims to revolutionize its operational efficiency by implementing a cutting-edge **Hotel Management System (HMS)**.

LuxuryStay envisions a modern, scalable, and user-friendly HMS to transform its operations, streamline workflows, enhance guest experiences, and provide insightful data for strategic decision-making.

## 🚀 Features  

### **Admin Panel** 🔒  
- **Dashboard Analytics**: Real-time occupancy rates, revenue trends.  
- **Role Management**: Assign roles (Admin/Receptionist/Housekeeping).  
- **Room Management**: Add, update, and remove rooms with availability tracking.  
- **Room Image Uploads**: Multer integration for storing room photos.  
- **Reservation Management**: View, approve, or cancel guest reservations.  
- **Guest Management**: Maintain guest records and booking history.  
- **Billing & Invoicing**: Generate invoices with breakdowns of room charges and additional services.  
- **Reports**: Generate PDF/Excel reports for audits and revenue tracking.  

### **User Panel** 👥  
- **Home Page**: Browse hotel details, amenities, and pricing.  
- **Room Booking System**: Search available rooms, select preferences, and confirm reservations.  
- **User Authentication**: Secure login & registration using JWT.  
- **Profile Management**: Update user details and view booking history.  
- **Invoice History**: View/download past invoices.  
- **Feedback & Reviews**: Submit ratings and reviews for rooms and services.  
- **Customer Support**: Contact admin for queries and issue reports.
